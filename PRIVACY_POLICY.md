# Privacy Policy for Ayyappa Seva

**Effective date: October 21, 2026**

Ayyappa Seva ("the App") is provided by VARK Technologies. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

Most devotional content is available without creating an account or providing personal information.

Authorized temple administrators sign in through Firebase Authentication. For those administrator accounts, we process:

- an email address; and
- a unique account identifier used to authenticate the administrator and control access to administrative features.

Administrators may create and manage temple events, announcements, and devotional-document information. That content is stored in Google Firebase so it can be displayed in the App.

## How We Use Information

Administrator account information is used only for app functionality, account authentication, security, and administration. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or use it to track users across apps or websites.

## Device Permissions

- **Notifications:** The App may request permission to schedule event reminders and, during a Mandala Deeksha, daily sunrise and sunset puja reminders and an Irumudi reminder. These reminders are scheduled locally on the device.
- **Approximate Location:** During a Mandala Deeksha, the App may request permission to use the device's approximate location while the App is in use. It is used only on the device to calculate the local sunrise and sunset times shown in puja reminders. Location is not uploaded, shared, sold, or used for tracking or advertising, and the App never uses background location. If permission is declined, the App uses the temple's location instead.
- **Photo Library:** The App may request permission to save a devotional wallpaper when the user chooses to download it. The App does not upload the user's photo library.

## Maladharana Registration

When a devotee registers for Maladharana, the name, contact details, and Deeksha dates entered in the form are stored only on the device so the App can show the 41-day Deeksha countdown and schedule reminders. Ending the Deeksha in the App deletes this information from the device.

## Service Providers

The App uses Google Firebase for administrator authentication and cloud-hosted app content. Google may process limited technical information needed to provide, secure, and maintain these services in accordance with its own privacy terms.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Administrator account information is retained while the account is active or as needed to provide and secure the service. An administrator may request account deletion by contacting us at the address below. Associated authentication information will be deleted unless retention is required for security or legal reasons.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.
